An agentic sales assistant for small B2B teams in Singapore, Malaysia and India. It finds the signal, checks the company is real, researches the lead and writes the email or WhatsApp. Nothing goes out until you approve.

The sales problem

  • Leads go cold in the CRM
  • Research eats the selling hours
  • Is this company even real?
  • Leads handed out by gut feel
  • Generic messages nobody answers

What Sales Mama does

  • Scans news, tenders, registries nightly
  • Researches with cited sources only
  • Checks any SG company against ACRA
  • Explainable scores, fair allocation
  • Email + WhatsApp, sent after you approve
