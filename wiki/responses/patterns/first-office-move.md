# first-office-move
## Structure
1. Subject: the move, in their words (where and when).
2. Opener: the cited move and its date from the account page.
3. Bridge: the day-one risk of a move (phones, WiFi and devices working before staff or patients arrive). If a `contract_ending` signal is also cited, add one line that the timing lines up with their review.
4. Offer: the recommended product(s), at most two, one sentence each.
5. CTA: one yes/no question tied to the move date.
6. Signature, then the unsubscribe line.
## Example (fictional company)
Subject: Your move to the new unit

Hi Lee,

I read that Acme Dental is moving to a larger unit nearby from 1 March.

Moves are when WiFi and devices tend to let you down on the first morning. Managed WiFi can be set up and tested in the new unit before you open there, and IT Care covers helpdesk and backups through the move.

Shall I send a one-page move checklist for the new unit?
## Rules
- At most 120 words. No links, no attachments.
- Use the move date and place exactly as the account page cites them.
- Don't mention the contract end date unless the account page cites it.
