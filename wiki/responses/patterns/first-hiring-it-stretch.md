# first-hiring-it-stretch
## Structure
1. Subject: the role they're hiring for, in their words (no `<ADV>` for a 1:1 researched email).
2. Opener: the cited hiring signal, with the role and what it covers (date from the account page).
3. Bridge: one line on the load that role is taking off ops today (the `it_stretch` hypothesis), as a question, not a claim.
4. Offer: the recommended product in one sentence, framed as cover alongside the hire, not instead of it.
5. Optional hook: a cited event or second signal, only if fresher than 30 days.
6. CTA: one yes/no question or a 15-minute call.
7. Signature, then the unsubscribe line.
## Example (fictional company)
Subject: Your IT & Systems Executive role

Hi Dana,

I saw Acme Physio is hiring an IT & Systems Executive to look after booking systems, devices and backups.
Until that role is filled, who's covering it for the clinics?

IT Care gives you a helpdesk, device setup and backups for a monthly fee, so the new hire can focus on the booking system.

Worth a 15-minute call next week?
## Rules
- At most 120 words in the body. No links, no attachments.
- Never imply they can't hire or that the hire is a mistake.
- Use only the role title and duties exactly as the account page cites them.
