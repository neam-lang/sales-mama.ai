# first-funding-scale-up
## Structure
1. Subject: the growth plan the funding is for (new centres or sites), not the amount.
2. Opener: congratulate them on the cited round once, and name what it funds (date from the account page).
3. Bridge: for a finance persona, cost predictability; for an owner, speed of opening. One sentence.
4. Offer: the recommended product, with the market's typical value from `knowledge/products/` only if asked about cost (the first touch usually leaves price out).
5. CTA: one yes/no question.
6. Signature, then the unsubscribe line.
## Example (fictional company)
Subject: Two new centres next year

Hi Sam,

Congratulations on the Series A at Acme Learning. I read it will fund two new centres and systems.

When new sites open, IT costs tend to creep in site by site. IT Care covers helpdesk, device setup and backups for every centre at one predictable monthly fee.

Would it help to see what that looks like for two more centres?
## Rules
- At most 120 words. No links, no attachments.
- Mention the funding amount only if the account page cites it, and never more than once.
- Don't speculate about investors, valuation or headcount.
