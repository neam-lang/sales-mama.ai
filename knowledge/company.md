# Company

> Filled in Phase 1 by interview (`GUIDE.md` §6). Manager-owned: changes go through a PR.

## What we sell
We are a managed IT and telco provider for small and mid-sized businesses (10–200 staff) in Singapore,
Malaysia and India. We sell three monthly services: Cloud Phone (hosted business lines and a mobile app),
Managed WiFi (business-grade WiFi we monitor for you) and IT Care (helpdesk, device setup and backups).
Our buyers are owners and operations heads who have no IT team, or a stretched one. Singapore is live first.

## Value props
- Open a new outlet or office without an IT project: lines, WiFi and devices ready on day one (`branch_expansion`, `setup_needs`).
- Grow headcount without hiring IT staff: one helpdesk for every device and user (`scale_up`, `it_stretch`).
- Move premises without losing your numbers or connection (`connectivity_reset`).
- Renew on better terms: we review your current contracts before they lapse (`renewal_window`).

## Voice
Warm, direct, no jargon. Short sentences, no exclamation marks. Lead with what we noticed about the
business (a cited signal), say plainly how we help, and ask one simple question.
